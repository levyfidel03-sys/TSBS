# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People considering a visit to The South Barbers who want to explore services and barbers and try a sample appointment flow.

## Product Purpose

Present the shop and its services and demonstrate an end-to-end booking journey. This is a UI-only prototype, not a live appointment service.

## Positioning

The prototype's defining mechanism is an interactive appointment journey that demonstrates service, barber, date, time, and customer-detail selection through a sample confirmation without creating a real appointment.

## Operating Context

Visitors explore the shop through a responsive, single-page website. The interface is intended for browser-based demonstration; all example content is maintained in frontend code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Visitors can explore haircut styles, services, grooming products, barber profiles, shop information, and a contact form.
- The appointment flow demonstrates service, barber, date, time, customer-detail, review, and confirmation stages.
- Booking, contact, and product interactions are demonstrations only. Do not transmit or persist customer data, create real appointments, send messages, or process purchases.
- Sample prices, contact information, barbers, availability, testimonials, and product listings are not verified facts. Do not present them as real or current.
- Keep the prototype frontend-only; it has no backend, database, or external service integration.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name The South Barbers.
- Preserve the supplied logo and photography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Supplied logo and photographs are in `logo.svg` and `pics/`.
- Frontend sample content and images are defined in `data.ts`.
- `The South Barbers — Prototype Outcomes.md` describes the intended prototype features and explicitly identifies demonstration content and frontend-only interactions.
- Prices, contact details, barbers, availability, and testimonials are sample content, not verified shop information.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy to explore the shop and understand the available sample services.
- Keep the appointment journey demonstrable without implying that a real booking was made.
- Never transmit or persist customer data through prototype interactions.
- Keep unverified business details clearly identifiable as examples and easy to replace.
